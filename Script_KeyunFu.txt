The big IT industries are changing C/C++ to Rust to resolve the memory safety issues that have bothered them for decades. Like the Distinguished engineer Galen Hunt from Microsoft said, "My goal is to eliminate every line of C and C++ from Microsoft by 2030". Even there is millions of codebase need to shift to rust the companies like Microsoft, AWS, meta still willing to process this enormous job. Why the memory safety so important? How are those companies going to process this job? In this presentation, we are going to talk about the key development and how it works, impact on software development and finally we will discuss the challenges and risks of it and the future outlook.
